# C08: The Settlement at the Edge

Levels **55–60** · Custom settlement near Cenarion Hold, Silithus · **12 quest records**

[Campaign index](README.md) · [Full entity catalog](catalog.md)

New settlement setup and Hidden Master-style training; no Tillers farm system or terrain-building phase.

**Chapter outcome:** A recognizable settlement and learned mechanic are established; the next lead crosses the Dark Portal.

Every objective and count below is the proposed adaptation. Source links identify the donor story; the listed givers, location, quantities and rewards are campaign design choices.

All quests award normal level-appropriate XP and money. This is a story route alongside ordinary leveling. Prerequisites below override display order; ALL and ANY mean exactly those joins.
